Lift Log

Lift Log is a full-stack powerlifting tracker where lifters log sessions, organize training into blocks, and track all-time PRs, weight-class progress, and estimated 1RMs.

It's built with vanilla JavaScript, Node/Express, and PostgreSQL, with JWT authentication, kg/lb conversion, CSV export, and a responsive layout. I designed the schema, built the REST API, and deployed it end to end.
